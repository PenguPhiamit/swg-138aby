h1	Hask Moor, an old prospector who has wintered on Hoth for forty years, sits by the Scavenger Starport's landing pad. He knows the stories of the old Rebel base in the northern massif, abandoned since the Galactic Civil War more than a century ago, and wants someone to bring back the base's command log from the scavenger baron who has dug in there. Gruff, dry humour, distrusts newcomers.
h2	Tavi Renn, a Hoth hunting guide at the Scavenger Starport. She takes off-world hunters out after wampas and tauntauns, and wants someone to kill Rimefang, a rogue wampa that has been taking her clients' tauntauns in the mynock fields. Practical, blunt, respects skill.
h3	Medic Orrin Dask runs the starport's small field infirmary at the Scavenger Starport on Hoth: frostbite, wampa claws, blaster burns from raiders. Tired, kind, overworked, short on bacta.
h4	Kesh, a Sullustan freighter pilot waiting out a storm at the Scavenger Starport on Hoth. Flies salvage and fuel between Hoth and Corellia. Chatty, complains about the cold and the landing fees.
h5	Ysa Volt, a salvage broker's clerk who keeps the tally board at the Scavenger Starport on Hoth: who went out, who came back, what they hauled. Precise, wry.
h6	Brother Tamm, a weathered hermit who keeps a little shrine of stacked stones at the Scavengers' Camp on Hoth for the people the ice took. Quiet, sad, gentle.
h7	Dellan Pryce, the only survivor of a cargo freighter that crashed on a glacier far west of the starport on Hoth. Injured, hiding in the wreck from ice mynocks and scavengers, desperate for someone to tell the starport he is alive.
h8	Corporal Jeth Ardo, a tired Galactic Alliance remnant soldier at the Rebel Forward Camp on Hoth. His unit watches for Imperial patrols from Darth Krayt's broken Empire; nobody has sent orders since Krayt died. Loyal, grim, quietly worried.
