# 1. Archivist (at the Memorial Road)

"The archives are incomplete, but the old legends whisper of a light kept hidden through the darkness."

"Look to the sites where the old masters once walked. The history is written in the stones."

"Seek the path of the Great Tree. If you find the right places, a silent shadow may appear."

# 2. A memorial record T'ra Saa left

"Life is a delicate bloom, precious and fleeting. Even as the shadows grow long over Ossus, we must protect the light that remains. I have tucked a small flicker away, deep within the earth, far from the reach of those who would consume it. Do not seek it for power, but for preservation. Let the peace of the forest guide you. Even in the silence of the end, life finds a way to endure."

# 3. Keeper, first meeting when the player is not ready

"You wander far from the path, traveler. The wind carries no answers for you yet."

"The sites of the old order remain silent. Return when you have truly listened to the world."

"Do not seek what is not ready to be found."

# 4. Keeper, to an Imperial

"My silence is a wall your kind will never climb."

# 5. Keeper, when the player is ready

"The echoes have led you here at last. You have walked the steps of the ancients."

"A long-lived soul once held a flame against the coming storm. T'ra Saa saw the beauty in the dimmest light."

"She hid this Ghostfire crystal to protect it from the hunger of war. Now, the time for its hiding is over."

# 6. The test

**Keeper:** "A flame can warm a hearth, or it can burn a forest to ash. How will you treat the light you find?"

*   **Option A (Mercy/Patience):** "I will guard it with care, letting it shine as it wishes."
    *   **Keeper:** "You understand that true strength is knowing when to hold back. You are ready."
*   **Option B (Force):** "I will use it to strike down my enemies!"
    *   **Keeper:** "Then you seek only destruction. The light will not serve a heart of fire."
*   **Option C (Greed):** "I want its power for myself!"
    *   **Keeper:** "The crystal is not a tool for your ambition. Leave this place."

# 7. Keeper, handing over the crystal

"Take this Ghostfire crystal. May its dim glow guide you through the mists."

"It was once a piece of a great soul's resolve. Carry it with honor."

# 8. Keeper, after the crystal is given

"Go now. The light has found a new keeper."

"The path is clear now. May the forest watch over you."
