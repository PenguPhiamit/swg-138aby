# Ossus: inspiration for Fable (Claude, 15 Oct 2026, at the owner's request)

The owner, 15 Oct about 10:45 PM: "give fable some more inspiration for ossus", and "more than two factions could be
cool too since there is more than 2 in universe". This is a seed list, not a design: take what works, drop the rest,
check every lore claim against Legends sources (Wookieepedia's Legends pages are the usual check; claims below marked
*check* are from memory). Settled decisions still rule: the ruins story (the academy fell in 130 ABY, Krayt's garrison
holds Ossus, a hidden sanctuary under the ruins), keep the layout, caves stay on the hillsides, no creature-level
changes, stock or catalogue-approved art, no downloads.

## 1. Ossus is a planet of layers

Every era left something on the ground. A visitor should be able to read four thousand years of history in one walk,
and the deeper you go (literally: down into the ruins), the older it gets.

| Layer | When | What it left | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Great Jedi Library | Old Republic, until about 4,000 BBY | the library and the old Jedi city around it; vaults, archives, statues, meditation terraces | ancient, monumental, half-buried, worn smooth |
| The Cron Supernova | about 3,996 BBY (the Great Sith War: a Sith ritual set off the Cron Cluster) | scorched plains, fused glass fields, collapsed domes; the Jedi evacuated what texts they could | a scar: shimmering glass, dead stone, strange calm |
| Those who stayed | 3,996 BBY to the present | the Ysanna, a Force-sensitive people descended from those left behind; the rooted Neti master (Ood Bnar) who guarded relics for millennia, *check* | tribal, living with ruins, half-remembered Jedi ways |
| The New Jedi Order's academy | rebuilt in the New Republic and Galactic Alliance years; the Jedi's home until 130 ABY *check dates* | the modern academy, landing fields, gardens, archives of the restored Order | bright, hopeful, now broken |
| The Ossus Project | about 122-127 ABY *check* | Yuuzhan Vong shapers and Jedi terraformed worlds; Ossus was the showcase; Sith sabotage of the project on other worlds started the Sith-Imperial War | living alien flora among Republic gardens, now sickened or feral |
| The massacre | 130 ABY | craters, scorched walls, wrecked fighters and walkers, memorials, the last stand | grief, silence, scorch marks (the owner's scorch-and-crater pass) |
| The occupation | 130-139 ABY | Krayt's garrison: checkpoints, a dig for holocrons, prison work camps (our era notes: coral-implanted prisoners, 137 ABY) | grim, watchful, temporary-looking but dug in |

## 2. Factions on Ossus: more than two

At 138-139 ABY, after Krayt's death, there are more than two sides (*check* the details of each in Legends):

- **Krayt's garrison (One Sith and Imperial holdouts).** They refuse the new order and keep digging for Jedi relics.
  Sith troopers, a Sith overseer, coral-implanted prisoners.
- **The Triumvirate** (the Galactic Federation Triumvirate formed in 138 ABY by Fel's Empire, the Galactic Alliance
  remnant and the Jedi, *check*). Scouts and Imperial Knights probing the garrison, a forward camp, a plan to retake the
  academy.
- **The hidden Jedi.** Very few, very careful; met only in the sanctuary under the ruins (the owner's rare Jedi).
- **The Ysanna.** Locals who belong to no side; guides, trackers, keepers of the old places. They decide whom to
  trust.
- **Scavengers and smugglers.** Relic hunters, black-market holocron dealers, a cartel buyer (Hutt kajidic money,
  which ties to the playable Hutt).
- **The Vong shapers' remnant** (only if it fits Nei Rin's Shapers, our 138 ABY voxyn decision): tending or
  reclaiming the Ossus Project's flora.

How this can work in Core3 without a full rewrite (a design question for you to answer, not a rule): the two GCW sides
stay for PvP flags, bases and the war (map them onto the era; see the existing notes before choosing), and the other
groups use pre-CU's NPC faction standing, the same system that tracks standing with the Hutts, Jabba, Nym, Borvo and
others. Standing with the Ysanna or the scavengers can open vendors, quests, safe camps and, eventually, the way to
the sanctuary. Say what Claude would need to build for each.

## 3. Points of interest: seeds

Place them on the census (`Bot-reports/launch-sim/ossus-census.md`): real ground, real neighbours.

- **The Library steps.** A grand stair leading to nothing: the library above it is gone. Statues with their faces worn
  off. A lore terminal reads fragments of a catalogue.
- **The Glass.** A field of fused, glossy ground from the supernova, where little grows. Odd creatures; mirages; a
  badge for crossing it.
- **The rooted master's grove.** A great dead tree the Ysanna revere: the Neti master who guarded the relics. A
  meditation spot; a Jedi-path hook.
- **Ysanna village** (or a few small camps). Hide tents among old stonework, a trader, guides, a storyteller who
  gives the planet's history as a quest.
- **The academy ruins** (the owner's scorch-and-crater pass). Bombed classrooms, a collapsed training hall, the
  landing field with wrecked fighters, a memorial wall for 130 ABY.
- **The garrison dig.** Cranes and floodlights over an excavation in the library vaults; prisoners working; a
  holocron-fragment heist.
- **The Ossus Project gardens.** Terraced gardens where Vong-shaped plants grew beside Republic ones, now feral or
  sick: living coral husks, swollen pods, glowing at night. A lair zone and a resource story.
- **The crash trail.** A Jedi transport that didn't make it out in 130 ABY; an escape-pod trail into the hills; who
  got away?
- **Smugglers' landing.** A hidden pad in the canyon country; relic dealers; the cartel buyer.
- **Triumvirate forward camp.** Small, quiet, watching the garrison; a recruiter and missions against the garrison.
- **Standing stones and meditation terraces.** Old Jedi places in the hills; seven of them could form a pilgrimage
  route that ends at the sanctuary's hidden door.
- **The sanctuary entrance.** Never marked: found through Ysanna trust, the pilgrimage stones, or a holocron map.

## 4. Building it from what we have

- **Stock SWG analogues:** Dantooine's Jedi temple ruins and Yavin 4's Massassi temples (ancient ruins);
  Dathomir's Nightsister stronghold and the Lok Imperial outpost (a dug-in garrison); Tatooine's Jawa and Tusken camps
  (Ysanna camps, with new dressing); crash sites and debris from the existing points of interest; Aurilia, the pre-CU
  Jedi village (a model for the sanctuary's role, not its look).
- **Our own notes:** `Documentation-support/starcraft-inspiration-vong-gree.md` (worldshaping zones, coral-implanted
  prisoners, living gear), `Documentation-support/krayt-raid-design.md`, `Documentation-support/legends-timeline-njo-legacy.md`,
  `Fable-resources3/lore/ossus-resource-map.md`, the content catalogue for MTG pieces.
- **Real places for mood** (look and feel only): Angkor's temples swallowed by trees; Petra's carved facades;
  Delphi's terraces; Pompeii's moments frozen under ash; the Chernobyl zone, where nature takes back an abandoned town;
  Iceland's black lava fields; Mesa Verde's cliff dwellings.

## 5. Gameplay hooks worth keeping in mind

- Exploration badges for the major sites (pre-CU had them); a "lost library" set of lore fragments to collect.
- A pilgrimage across the standing stones as the Jedi-path quest on this planet.
- Garrison patrols that sweep for Force-sensitives, and a rumour system ("a Jedi was seen near the grove").
- Night changes: the Project flora glows; the glass field shimmers; the garrison's floodlights.
- Settlements on the flat squares, framed in the story as returning settlers and salvagers: the planet is
  being reclaimed.
