## What the original fights actually were

**Horsemen.** Lingering Harmony and Embraced Chaos are vulnerable to melee; Promised Vengeance and Unquenchable Wrath to ranged. The wrong attack type strengthens them. They are a stage of a longer heroic, not four mounted enemies or an emulator invention. The NGE encounter also has cultists, other bosses and a final Exar Kun/Massassi Blade sequence. Port the encounter idea to pre-CU weapons, HAM and threat rules; do not import NGE profession assumptions. Wookieepedia dates the characters/heroic to 2008 and describes enslaved archaeological students; the gameplay guide identifies the four combat roles. [Lingering Harmony, Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lingering_Harmony_of_Exar_Kun), [original fight guide](https://swg.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun). Exar Kun's definitive later banishment makes a contemporary reappearance an adaptation requiring a stated premise. [Exar Kun, Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Exar_Kun/Legends).

**Death Troopers.** The 19 outdoor names in the published list run from level 5 to 95: Fenn Kay'lay, Vil Burren, Jawiin Danod, Salek Panaka, Snotharr, Navik Geelo, Dugo Evnul (the page also spells it Envul), Adol Mantid, Tal Freykaa, Kallaova, Soon Dom, Ool Awmod, Jaina Nassin, Wertsnik, Wesk Kit'pay, Ghianna Hayde, Chak Krist, Treena Moldovia and Vina Maljazara. Cornburr is an additional facility boss on that page, not a twentieth entry in the 5–95 outdoor sequence. The area had survivor camps, a prison/research facility, infection-related quests and collection rewards. “19 bosses” therefore describes a regional collection, not 19 raid-scale fights. Do not assume the 19 appearance files map one-to-one by filename to these names. [Roster](https://swg.fandom.com/wiki/Undead_Boss), [Quarantine Zone history and October 2009 opening](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quarantine_Zone_%28Dathomir%29), [a named victim's Legends history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Salek_Panaka).

**Necrosis.** The original encounter uses an activation crystal and Treun Lorn, followed by Necrosis with two NK-3 bodyguards. It is a combat droid built with Grievous's recovered body; its distinctive possible rewards included Bane's Heart and the Soulless One deed. Those rewards are historical reference, not a proposed promise of ships/JTL functionality here. Our Necrosis template has lightsaber attacks but **empty loot**, and NK-3 has **empty attacks and loot**. Neither constitutes the original activation quest. [N-K Necrosis, Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/N-K_Necrosis). The article's behind-the-scenes month conflicts with the expansion's own May release date; use the [expansion article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars_Galaxies%3A_Rage_of_the_Wookiees) for dating.

**Wildlife and the ancient dragon.** Ordinary krayts and mutant rancors are repeatable hunting content; named world bosses are an additional layer. The stock mutant-rancor cave predates the later Witches of Dathomir quest retelling. Our current cave screenplay spawns mutant/enraged/ancient rancor variants; it is not the later NGE quest chain. [Original cave, Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mutant_Rancor_Cave), [krayt biology, Legends](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Krayt_dragon/Legends). The other server's Ancient Greater fight adds action suppression and punishments for spending action; our stock ancient has area combo/knockdown and our rotation's HAM multiplier instead. There is no basis to label them identical. [Ancient Greater description](https://swglegends.com/wiki/index.php?title=Ancient_Greater_Krayt_Dragon).

**Unkajo, only if that is the intended name.** The SOE Hoth fight followed clearing Wampa Valley and used a combat barrier, freezing attacks, thawing opportunities and waves of wampas. The Legends biography links the enlarged creature to Kyrisa's experimentation. For our period, a new giant wampa in the abandoned Hoth setting preserves the concept without replaying the Battle of Hoth as a contemporary event. [Legends biography](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unkajo/Legends), [encounter mechanics](https://swglegends.com/wiki/index.php?title=Unkajo), [Wampa Valley](https://swglegends.com/wiki/index.php?title=Wampa_Valley).
