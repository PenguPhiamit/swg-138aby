# KOTOR I/II mod reuse and author permissions audit

Research by ChatGPT, 13 October 2026. Shared with the owner and Claude. Companion: [technical feasibility](KOTOR-to-SWG-and-open-assets-feasibility.md).

Additional pass: [30 more releases and further author statements](KOTOR-mod-reuse-audit-additional-authors.md). Combined numbered inventory: 56 releases/resources. No import clearance implied.

## Scope and how to read this

This is a researched shortlist, not a census of every KOTOR mod ever published. It covers 26 releases/resources and the authors and principal contributors identified below. Public release pages, creator profiles, separate permission posts, historical forums and archive routes were checked. No packages downloaded, assets extracted, authors contacted or content imported. Package readmes and individual file provenance remain unchecked unless reproduced on the public page.

**A permission to use a creator's contribution does not establish permission for stock BioWare/Obsidian art or every contributor.** Owning both games, LAN use and willingness to pay do not establish those missing grants. Broad grants are useful evidence; an explicit other-games conversion setting is stronger evidence for our proposed SWG use. Missing text means unverified, not a prohibition. Permission-required terms are clear restrictions, not missing terms. No complete package is cleared for SWG import by this audit.

Evidence: **P** = primary page/post readable; **I** = indexed creator/site text readable but direct page failed; **S** = secondary report of another author's permission. Statements below are paraphrases, not substitute licences. Keep the original licence/readme and URL with any eventual selected asset.

## RedHawke site recovery and permission trail

**Original address:** `http://www.citlink.net/~redhawke/`, with mods index `http://www.citlink.net/~redhawke/mods.html`. Original address survives in [Xia's preserved author readme](https://www.gamefront.com/games/knights-of-the-old-republic/file/xia-terashai-set) and [older forum discussion](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/516675-star-wars-knights-of-the-old-republic/51890241).

**Later caretaker mirror:** `http://www.jumpstationz.com/RedHawke/`, with `mods.html`. [The 2017–2020 preservation thread](https://deadlystream.com/topic/5623-reuploading-the-jumpstationz-mods/) names it directly. ChAiNz announced closure planned for 11 April 2020 and asked for preservation on Deadly Stream. Thus the missing site has a documented closure/preservation trail, not merely an unknown URL.

**What is recoverable now:** Deadly Stream's [JumpStationZ archive account](https://deadlystream.com/profile/52530-jumpstationz/content/?type=downloads_file), RedHawke release pages such as [ORD Mandell](https://deadlystream.com/files/file/1627-redhawkes-ord-mandell/), public historical release/readme text, and the caretaker posts. No archived files downloaded.

**What the permissions say:** The preservation thread contains caretaker authorization for rehosting and blanket permissions, alongside staff's explicit distinction between hosting and modification. In [February 2023 posts](https://deadlystream.com/profile/8920-chainz2da/content/), ChAiNz says he had not heard from RedHawke for years, transferred custody to Deadly Stream in 2020, and describes his former credit/readme rule while deferring to staff and RedHawke's wishes. This is useful caretaker evidence, but not a recovered direct RedHawke-wide grant explicitly covering SWG. Xia's author-uploaded conversion setting is more concrete evidence for that specific package. Do not collapse these different scopes.

**Archive limitation:** A Wayback CDX request for the Citlink mods index and two nearest-snapshot requests for Citlink/JumpStationZ failed in the research browser. No snapshot timestamp or recovered page contents verified. [Citlink archive calendar](https://web.archive.org/web/*/http://www.citlink.net/~redhawke/mods.html) and [mirror archive calendar](https://web.archive.org/web/*/http://www.jumpstationz.com/RedHawke/mods.html) are lookup routes, not verified captures. The original live sites are not being recommended as working downloads.

**Historical context:** RedHawke's [2007 forum response](https://www.lucasforumsarchive.com/thread/179101-looking-to-extensively-mod-probably-will-need-help) upheld then-current community restrictions on cross-game ports. That is historical forum policy, not proof of current publisher rules or a licence revocation. ORD Mandell deliberately differs from canonical Ord Mantell: [RedHawke explained the naming himself](https://www.lucasforumsarchive.com/thread/154351-top-ten-kotor-1-mods). Its rooms reuse Korriban modules; do not assume a complete independently modelled planet.

## Practical next selection

1. For an author-made prop, investigate **InSidious holocrons** first, then **DarthParametric's untextured Lethisk mesh**. They offer relatively small, documented components, although actual provenance and conversion are untested.
2. For explicit cross-game permission examples, retain **Juhani robes and Xia set** as evidence. Their meshes/textures may still derive from stock content; permissions are not a technical readiness rating.
3. For environments, investigate **Quanon's resource modules** and the exact BoS contribution inventory. Silveredge9's own broad grant is verified; Quanon's original resource wording and other contributors still need verification.
4. Avoid starting with full TSLRCM/M4-78/K1R imports. They combine stock material, contributor chains and permission-required terms with substantial SWG scripting/navigation work.
5. Before any selected import, record exact release/version/files/hash, original terms, all contributors, whether content is original or stock-derived, cross-game and live-server scope, credit/readme requirements, notification/contact obligations and monetization conditions. Seek owner approval before contacting anyone. Willingness to pay is an option for a future agreement, not an existing grant.

## Research trail and outstanding work

UM's archive-aware web research method was used. Representative queries: `RedHawke citlink permissions`; `Does anyone have contact information to Redhawke`; `ChAiNz.2da blog permissions`; `Silveredge9 blanket permissions`; `Quanon Big Sellout`; `svösh blanket permission`; `Dark Hope permissions`; and author-name + permissions searches for the register. Gated/unavailable pages were not bypassed; no private profiles/messages accessed. No game conversion or in-game testing was performed.
