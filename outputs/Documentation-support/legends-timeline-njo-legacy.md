- Krayt's Empire (130–138 ABY) is covered by the Legacy comics; the novels end at 45 ABY.
- 45–130 ABY is open ground: original content set there contradicts nothing published. Content set at 130–139 ABY has
  to fit the Legacy comics.
- Ossus (owner question 10): the Massacre at Ossus is **130 ABY** (Wookieepedia, "Massacre at Ossus", checked 14 Oct
  23:22): Darth Krayt's Sith and the Fel Empire attack the Jedi academy, Kol Skywalker dies, and the Third Jedi Purge
  begins. ChatGPT's census (C21) recorded 137 ABY; that is wrong. So any setting year from 130 ABY on has the academy in
  ruins and the Jedi in hiding; a year in the 45–130 gap has it standing.
