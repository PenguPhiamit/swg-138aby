# KOTOR author audit: additional research

ChatGPT — 13 October 2026. Owner requested roughly ten more minutes of author research. Continues [the first 26-release audit](KOTOR-mod-reuse-audit.md); release numbers continue at 27. These are 30 additional release/resource entries, not 30 newly cleared packages or 30 distinct authors.

Public pages and author statements only. No downloads, extraction, imports, messages, payments or server changes. **No complete package is cleared for SWG import.** A creator's grant covers their contribution; stock game art, other artists, voices and texture source material remain separate. Conversion is untested. Evidence **P** means readable primary page/post; **I** means indexed creator text or a primary site listing; **S** means another person's account of a grant. Missing terms are not a prohibition.

## Best leads after this pass

- **Small objects:** existing InSidious holocrons, Seph6 holocron, DarthParametric's original Lethisk mesh. Select a file and prove its provenance before conversion.
- **Environment work:** Sleheyron demo and djh269 hut resource have readable, broad grants for creator contributions. Need dependency inventory; neither is a ready SWG planet.
- **Armour:** Praetorian package has explicit other-games conversion terms, with both credited creators identified. Original mesh/texture chain still unverified.
- **Species:** Kel Dor material is a real technical resource with credit-based author terms, but has layered stock/other-mod dependencies and animation limitations. It is not evidence that SWG can simply load KOTOR rigs.
- **Textures:** ShiningRedHD is a stronger author-permission candidate than the audited Vurt or Selphadur packs, but all stock-derived upscales retain original-game rights questions.

### Final checks from this pass

- **Effix: broader statement found.** His [own post listing](https://deadlystream.com/profile/28754-effix/content/?type=forums_topic_post) records a 1 November 2025 statement that his latest policy permits using his creations with credit. The [original thread](https://deadlystream.com/topic/2632-wip-kainzorus-primes-robe-model-reskin-project-and-more/) also displays the post. This upgrades the earlier first audit's limited per-release evidence to a verified broader creator policy. It still does not license original-game assets or other artists' work.
- **Leilukin: mixed portfolio confirmed.** [Exile's Trial Overlay Removal](https://www.nexusmods.com/kotor2/mods/1184) permits reuse in own mods, credit appreciated, and prohibits Workshop upload. Therefore the restrictive Kreia/Twi'lek cases above are not a whole-portfolio judgement. This dialogue patch also depends on TSLRCM; creator permission and that dependency remain separate.
- **DarthParametric Kel Dor chain improved.** His [own file listing](https://deadlystream.com/profile/15735-darthparametric/content/?type=downloads_file) describes a custom head/hands with modified stock bodies and expressly permits incorporation with credit while banning standalone redistribution. This supplies upstream author-grant evidence for entry 49; other textures, stock bodies and Bao-Dur animations remain unresolved.
