## Critical deviations and ordering

1. Our snapshot has a documented starport at `(0,-2000)`; the three Bellum outposts have moved. Use Claude's mapped landing point `(20.3,-1982.2)` with height still to verify. Preserve reverse travel before content. See [port log](../hoth-port-log.md).
2. Four cave world positions match the current log, but the source screenplay also includes additional cave headers and numerous raw cell IDs. Matching geometry positions do not prove IDs/portal/floor coordinates match. Compare every spawned cell to current snapshot objects, not just the first four caves. Omit unmatched sites until generated from our snapshot.
3. Normal source tauntauns start at level 65; wampas at 90; several resist entries exceed 100, and Rimeclaw has chanceHit 95 (other creatures roughly 0.6–1.1). These values are **source facts**, not endorsed local balance or evidence of the target engine's interpretation. Keep them in the inventory, request a deliberate balance decision, and begin with observation-only smoke tests.
4. No silent whole-server reward migration: `bg_token_group`, endgame schematics and custom vehicle/armor/decor families are not prerequisites for drawing Hoth. Defer them, use a separately reviewed small local loot table and verify actual receipt. Cave rewards call `giveHothBossRewards`, defined in the bonus-loot file; registry order and callback timing must be checked.
5. Port in slices: registration/travel → ordinary creature definitions → verified outdoor and cave spawns → lairs/missions → resources → independent boss/reward slice. For each: parse/load, inspect in-game, one success/refusal/persistence check where applicable, then cross-review before owner-approved live promotion.

## Lore constraints (Legends)

[Hoth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hoth/Legends) and its [system](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hoth_system/Legends) are in the Anoat sector, Outer Rim, grid **K-18**. [Wampas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wampa/Legends) are predators; [tauntauns](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tauntaun/Legends) are adapted mounts/prey. Cave encounters and supply/survival objectives fit those facts. Bellum's named wampa bosses and glacial rancor are server-specific inventions; do not present them as established lore.

[Echo Base](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Echo_Base/Legends) was constructed in 2 ABY and destroyed in 3 ABY. Choose the server's intended date before writing an active-base or ruins campaign; the current MTG starport and four caves do not themselves constitute Echo Base. Extra Imperial landings or camps require original placement, not copied movie imagery. Wookieepedia search-index extracts were readable; direct page access reported robots restrictions. Facts are cited, text is original; no images/text imported.
