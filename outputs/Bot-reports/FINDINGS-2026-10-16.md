## Game bugs or likely bugs (they affect real players)

1. **Ranged characters can't destroy mission lairs.**
   - **Answered 10 PM (C61, `Documentation-support/C61-ranged-lairs.md`): BotPop-only, not a game bug.** BotPop never gave its bots their species skill, which grants the CDEF weapon certifications. `CombatManager::calculateDamage` cuts an uncertified weapon's damage to a lair to 5-10, and the lair's creatures heal it faster than that. Real players get the skill at creation. Fixed in dev `fe399424` (run 17); the 7 AM report shows whether marksmen now finish missions.
   - Evidence:
     - In runs 4-12, ranged bots reached 1,062 lairs and finished none.
     - In run 13, marksmen took 3,087 missions and finished 2. Scouts took 489 and finished 1. Brawlers finished 46%.
     - Marksmen find the lair (3,049 times) and stand 5 m from it for 15-20 minutes until the mission aborts.
   - Ruled out: line of sight blocked by the lair's own model. `CollisionManager::checkLineOfSight` skips the target object.
   - Next:
     - Dev `665bea04` logs a `lair_check` row once a minute at the lair: distance, line of sight, weapon, ranged or not, attackable, posture, and the lair's damage so far.
     - The next run says which check fails, or whether shots land and do no damage.
     - Then a test with a real ranged character.
2. **New characters get missions they can't finish.**
   - The terminal offers lairs within the player's level ±5 (stock Core3, `MissionManagerImplementation.cpp:1896-1902`).
   - Level-2 characters took mostly level 4-6 missions and finished 3% of them.
   - Finish rates by gap: 93% at -3, 44% at 0, 12% at +2, 2% at +4.
   - **Owner decision:** narrow the offer for low levels (for example level -5 to +2 below level 10), or keep stock.
3. **The shuttle network has never been tested at scale.**
   - BotPop's "travel" teleports bots (`switchZone`). It never buys a ticket or boards a shuttle, so run 13 shows "Travel: none".
   - Gap 9 of the [test plan](../Documentation-support/functional-test-plan.md).
   - Next: a travel behaviour that uses the ticket terminal and the shuttle.
4. **Nal Hutta is reachable only from Tatooine.** This is by design of the first look. The owner found it unreachable from Corellia.
   - Add more routes if wanted.
   - The server also loaded no walking mesh for Bilbousa ("0 nav areas loaded for hutta"); this only matters for bots.
