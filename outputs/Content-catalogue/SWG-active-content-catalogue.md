Updated 13 October 2026. Sentinel’s Republic has been removed from the active research/import plan at the user’s request. Historical records remain available.

This catalogue contains 102 research entries, including 18 ground worlds, 3 stations and 40 additional species/character candidates. The species count includes 37 legacy EiF source definitions and three later options; it is not a claim of 40 installed or currently selectable additions.
