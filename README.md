# A private Star Wars Galaxies sandbox

A personal, non-commercial project: a private SWGEmu (Core3) server for playing and preserving pre-Combat-Upgrade Star
Wars Galaxies, with new content set in the Legends era around 138–139 ABY, after Darth Krayt's fall. So far that means
the planets Hoth and Ossus, vehicles and items backported from Mod the Galaxy, new creatures, and a squad of test bots
that log in through the real game protocol to check what works for every profession.

## Goals

- Keep the pre-CU game as it was, and finish what was left thin or broken (Image Designer, Politician and others).
- Add places and story that fit the setting, in our own words.
- Make testing cheap and honest: bots that play each profession and report what works and what doesn't.

## Transparency

- Most of the engineering and writing here is done by AI agents (Claude, ChatGPT/Codex and a local model) working
  under my direction. I set the priorities, test in game and make the calls. Their task board, notes and reviews stay
  in this repository on purpose, so anyone can see how each piece was made and checked.
- It builds on other people's open work under their own licences: SWGEmu's Core3, Mod the Galaxy, TortoiseBots (with
  its author's permission) and Wookieepedia for lore (our text is our own). Credits and sources are kept in the repo.
- No leaked or not-for-distribution code or assets are used, and no game client files are included.
